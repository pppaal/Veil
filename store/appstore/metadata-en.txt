[Promotional Text]
An end-to-end encrypted messenger with no phone number, no email and no real name. Only you and the people you talk to can read your messages.

[Description]
Veil is a messenger you sign up for with a handle, not a phone number or email.

How it works
• No phone number, no email and no real name.
• Every message is end-to-end encrypted on-device. Servers never see your messages in plaintext.
• There is no account recovery. If you lose your phone, you lose the account and its messages.

Encryption
• X25519 key exchange and AES-256-GCM encryption
• Ed25519 device signatures
• HKDF-SHA256 key derivation

Features
• One-to-one and group chats
• Typing indicators, online status and optional read receipts
• App lock with a PIN or biometrics
• Move your account to a new phone
• English

Privacy
• No ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics.
• Designed to meet Korea's PIPA requirements.

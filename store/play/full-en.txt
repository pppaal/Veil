An end-to-end encrypted messenger with no phone number, no email and no real name.
Veil is a chat app where even the server can't read your messages. Instead
of finding people by phone number, you give them your handle.

■ How it works
• Sign up with a handle. No phone number, no email, no real name.
• Every message is end-to-end encrypted on-device. Servers never see your messages in plaintext.
• There is no account recovery. If you lose your phone, you lose the account and its messages.

■ Encryption
• X25519 key exchange and AES-256-GCM encryption
• Ed25519 device signatures
• HKDF-SHA256 key derivation
• Messages are encrypted on your device and stored on the server only as ciphertext until they expire, at most 30 days.

■ Features
• One-to-one and group chats
• Typing indicators, online status and optional read receipts
• App lock with a PIN or biometrics
• Move your account to a new phone
• English

■ Who it's for
• Anyone who wants to message without giving out a phone number
• Journalists, activists and anyone else who needs private conversations

■ Privacy
• No ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics.
• The privacy policy says what the server stores and links the published list of every field it keeps.
• Designed to meet Korea's PIPA requirements.
• Open source: anyone can read the code.

■ One phone at a time
Veil runs on one phone at a time. Moving to a new phone moves your handle to it and signs the old phone out. Your message history does not move.
